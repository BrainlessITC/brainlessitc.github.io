BrainlessITC PDF Invoices & Packing Slips for Woo

Create PDF invoices and packing slips for your WooCommerce orders — with safe sequential invoice numbers, email attachments, bulk printing and secure customer downloads.

Get started WordPress.org page ↗ Pro add-on

What it does

The plugin creates professional PDF invoices and packing slips for WooCommerce orders. Everything is generated on your own server — no external service, account or API key is needed. Invoices get a sequential number that is issued safely (never twice), they can be attached to WooCommerce emails automatically, and customers can download their invoices from My Account.

PDF invoicesLogo, store details, tax ID, billing and shipping address, SKUs, variations, discounts, shipping, fees, taxes and totals. Classic and Modern layouts.
Packing slipsShipping address, items with SKU and variation details, quantity to ship and a "packed" checkbox — never prices.
Safe numberingNumbers are issued in a locked database transaction with unique keys: no duplicates, no silent changes, every number in the register.
Email attachmentsAttach the invoice (and packing slip) to the WooCommerce emails you choose.
Bulk printingBulk actions on the orders list create one PDF for all selected orders.
Customer downloadsDownload buttons in My Account; guests get a signed link that only works for their order and expires.
