Giftora Pro

Everything stores ask for most: custom amounts, scheduled delivery, designs, PDF gift cards, expiry, a gift card wallet and powerful admin tools.

Features

Custom amountsCustomers enter their own amount within your minimum and maximum, next to your preset amounts.
Scheduled deliveryCustomers choose the day the gift card is emailed — perfect for birthdays. Set the time of day in the settings.
DesignsUpload card images in Gift Cards > Designs (birthday, thank you, holidays…). Customers pick one; it is used in the email and PDF.
PDF gift cardsA printable PDF with design, amount, code and a QR code is attached to the email and downloadable.
Expiry & remindersValidity in months, per store or per product. Reminder emails before a card with a balance expires.
My Account walletCustomers see their cards and balances, add cards to their account, use them in one click and download PDFs.
Several recipientsSend the same gift card to many people in one purchase — each gets their own card.
Physical gift cardsShipped instead of emailed; the codes are shown on the order for printing.
Redemption rulesMinimum order amount, categories that cannot be paid with gift cards, and whether cards pay for shipping.
Bulk toolsGenerate hundreds of cards at once, export and import gift cards as CSV.
Refund to gift cardGive store credit from the order screen: a refund is recorded and a new gift card is emailed.
ReportsIssued, sold, redeemed, outstanding balance (liability) and expired value, by month.

Upgrading and downgrading

Giftora Pro is a complete plugin that replaces the free edition. When you activate Pro, the free plugin is deactivated automatically — all gift cards, balances, history and settings are used as they are. If your license ends, or you go back to the free plugin, nothing is lost: every gift card keeps working, scheduled gift cards are still delivered, and your Pro settings come back when you upgrade again.
