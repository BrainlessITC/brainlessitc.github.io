BrainlessITC Gift Cards for WooCommerce

Sell digital gift cards: customers choose an amount and write a message, the recipient gets the code by email and redeems it on the classic or block checkout, keeping any balance for later. BrainlessITC Gift Cards Pro adds custom amounts, scheduled delivery, designs, PDF gift cards, expiry, a My Account wallet, bulk tools and reports.
